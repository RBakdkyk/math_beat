# 2nd grade (כיתה ב׳) — curriculum and game ideas

For the 7-year-old. Written 2026-11-02.

Source: the Ministry of Education 2nd grade math program (18 pages, updated Nov 2025):
https://meyda.education.gov.il/files/Mazkirut_Pedagogit/math/primary-school/math2023/Newprogramgrade2.pdf

## What 2nd grade covers

| Topic | Hours | In short |
|---|---|---|
| Adding, subtracting, times and division up to 100 | ~65 | Two-digit + and − in her head, column sums last; first times/division with 2, 4, 5, 10 as equal groups; money problems |
| Numbers up to 1,000 | ~20 | Hundreds, tens, ones; counting in jumps of 10, 20, 50, 100; even and odd; number patterns |
| Length | ~10 | Centimeters, meters, distance around a shape (perimeter) |
| 3-D shapes and volume | ~10 | Cube, box, pyramid, cylinder and so on |
| Data | ~10 | Tables, bar charts, picture charts |
| Flat shapes | ~8 | Types of triangle, right angles |
| Clock | ~5 | Whole and half hours, how long something takes |

The plan asks for understanding over speed. Examples it gives:
- 56 is also "4 tens and 16 ones"
- do sums in your head, and talk about how you did them
- more coins doesn't always mean more money

## Game ideas

Her current game, ארץ המספרים, only goes up to 30. These go further and keep the same gentle style: no "wrong", hints after a mistake, a skip button and stickers.

1. **חנות הממתקים (Candy shop).** Pay exact prices with Israeli coins and notes, then count change. Covers + and − up to 100, tens and ones, and money. Trick question: "who has more money, 5 coins or 2 coins?"
2. **קפיצות הצפרדע (Frog jumps).** Get a frog from 34 to 72 on a number line with jumps of +10 and +1; fewer jumps earn more stickers. Teaches the in-your-head strategy; jumping backwards is subtraction.
3. **גינת הכפל (Times garden).** Plant flowers in rows (3 rows of 5). Turning the garden sideways shows 3×5 = 5×3. Covers first times/division with 2, 4, 5, 10.
4. **בית המספרים (Number house up to 1,000).** Build numbers from bundles of 100, 10 and 1, swap one ten for ten ones, and place numbers on a 0–1,000 line.
5. **השעון של היום שלי (My day clock).** Set the clock hands for her own day ("school at 8, dance class at 4:30") and work out how long things take.

**Recommended:** the candy shop or the frog. Both cover the biggest unit (65 hours), and the shop feels most like play.

Shapes and 3-D solids are left out on purpose: the plan says to learn them with real objects, which works better than a screen.
